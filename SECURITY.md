# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately, not in a public issue: open this repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Only the maintainers see the report, and we answer through the same private advisory.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it. Please give us a reasonable time to fix it before you disclose it publicly.

## What we have checked

TorFinder is reviewed against OWASP ASVS 5.0 (Level 1 and Level 2 in full) and the OWASP Top 10 (2025). The reports, the threat model and the test evidence are in [docs/security](docs/security/).
